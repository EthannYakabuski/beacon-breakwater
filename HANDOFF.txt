PLAY AND IMPLEMENTED PACKAGE
Aim by dragging over water. Hold the beam on a boat until guidance completes;
guide it home before its timer expires. Use the free Foghorn, pause and Resume.
Thirty authored watches across six coasts, Endless/Daily, gifts/quests, equipment,
keeper progression, banked saves/backup and records are implemented. Supplied
native plugins use sample ad IDs; PGS has 20 achievements/four boards with blank,
disabled real-ID mappings. Store illustrations/copy/icons and nine reviewed
normal-play phone/logical-tablet screenshots are included with their notices.
