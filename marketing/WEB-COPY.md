# Beacon Breakwater — sweep a light, save a fleet

The coast is quiet until the first sail appears. From a little lighthouse above
the breakwater, your beam is the one reliable line through tide and storm. Bring
each vessel home, then turn back toward the next signal on the horizon.

**Sweep a light. Save a fleet.** One finger moves the beam. A steady hold establishes
guidance; a full green ring sends that vessel toward harbor. Read urgency and make
small, satisfying choices as the fleet arrives.

**Every boat is worth bringing home.** Quick skiffs, heavy cargo boats and emergency
vessels ask for different priorities. Thirty curated watches across six coasts
combine traffic, shoals and weather, with endless and daily watches alongside.

**Build your beacon, one watch at a time.** Spend earned coins on a wider lens,
faster focus and better guidance. Keeper levels, daily goals, local achievements
and personal records make the next light visible.

Technical: Godot4.5.1 Compatibility, portrait Android primary, single-threaded Web,
local checksummed saves, optional native Android services, touch/mouse and keyboard
development controls. Web plays offline after assets load when the host permits
it; no service worker or guaranteed cached-offline installation is claimed.
Browser audio, WebGL and embedding need actual supported-browser QA. Native
rewarded assistance is unavailable on Web and never faked. Backend/signing/store
and human playtesting remain owner work.
