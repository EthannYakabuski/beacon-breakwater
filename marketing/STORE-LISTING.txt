TITLE
Beacon Breakwater

SHORT DESCRIPTION (under80characters)
Sweep a lighthouse beam. Guide a fleet home through tide, fog and storm.

CATEGORY
Games / Arcade
Tags: Casual; Single player; Offline; Portrait; Rescue; Strategy

LONG DESCRIPTION
A small lighthouse. A coast worth keeping.

The first boat is a quiet shape on the horizon. Sweep your light onto its sail,
hold the beam steady, and watch its guidance ring fill. A green ring means a safe
route home. Then another boat appears, a current turns, and an emergency cutter
needs your light first. Every watch is a little story about bringing people home.

ONE LIGHT, CLEAR CHOICES
Drag anywhere across the water to aim your beam. Prioritize quick skiffs, patient
cargo sailers and urgent rescue vessels. The free foghorn slows danger when the
water gets busy, and recharges as you play. You can pause whenever you need.

SIX COASTS TO KEEP
Follow thirty curated watches from welcoming Lantern Bay to stormy Northlight
Cape. Learn the beam in two opening watches, then read new traffic patterns,
shoals, cross-currents and weather. Clear a watch, improve its stars or settle
into an endless score chase. A daily watch offers a fresh reason to return.

BUILD A BETTER BEACON
Rescues earn coins and keeper experience. Widen the lens, sharpen the focus and
buy boats more guidance time. First clears, perfect watches, daily goals and a
seven-day login gift make progress visible. Level-ups pay a clear coin bonus.
Track twenty achievements and four leaderboard metrics with optional Play Games
support when configured. Your personal records and harbor save work offline.

OPTIONAL HELP, HONEST RULES
Android advertising supports the game. The harbor has a reserved menu banner,
and an interstitial may appear between every two naturally finished watches.
An optional earned rescue flare guides the boats currently on the water once
per watch. It marks that watch assisted so your unassisted endless best stays
meaningful. Ads require availability; ordinary play never requires an ad.
Web and offline play do not simulate advertising rewards.

Designed for portrait play and a single sweeping finger. No purchases or paid
progression are implemented. Local progress is saved on this device. Production
service configuration and final store disclosures remain owner release work.
